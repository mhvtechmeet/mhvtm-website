About MHV TechMeet

The Mid-Hudson Valley TechMeet brings together college students, faculty, researchers, and industry professionals to share ideas, showcase technology projects, and build connections across the Hudson Valley.

The TechMeet provides college students with opportunities to showcase their work, connect with peers and professionals, and explore emerging technologies in a collaborative environment.

Through poster presentations, discussions, and networking, TechMeet creates a forum where students can share their work with a broader technical community and learn from others.

Our Beginnings

The Mid-Hudson Valley TechMeet was established in 2024 to create a regional forum connecting college students with peers, academics, and industry professionals.

The inaugural TechMeet poster session was held on November 8, 2024 at Bard College in Annandale-on-Hudson, New York.

The second edition was held on November 7, 2025 at Marist University in Poughkeepsie, New York.

Our Mission

TechMeet is designed to foster collaboration across colleges, universities, and industry throughout the Mid-Hudson Valley.

By giving students a platform to present their projects and engage with professionals and academics, the event helps build connections that can lead to new ideas, collaborations, and career opportunities.
